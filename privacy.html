DOT2. PRIVACY NOTICE

Your privacy.

Last updated: 27 October 2026

Who we are

Dot2. is the trading name of DOT2. LTD, a company registered in England and Wales under company number 14165112. Our registered office is 124 City Road, London, England, EC1V 2NX. DOT2. LTD is the controller responsible for the personal information described in this notice. For questions, requests or concerns, email support@dot2.co.uk or write to our registered office.

Business enquiries and quotes

When you contact us, we receive the details you choose to send, such as your name, work email, company, order quantity, design requirements and correspondence. If you go ahead with an order, this may also include artwork, delivery details and invoicing information. Please share only what is needed for your request.

We use these details to answer questions, prepare quotes, discuss designs and manage orders. Our lawful basis is our legitimate interest in responding to enquiries and managing business relationships. Where you are personally entering a contract with us, we also use the contract basis for steps you request before a contract and for fulfilling it. We retain records where needed to meet legal obligations.

The business page’s quote helper prepares an email in your browser. Preparing it does not submit an enquiry to Dot2.; we receive your request only when you send the email. Requesting a quote does not sign you up to marketing.

Business outreach

Our current business outreach campaign uses general company mailboxes published on relevant corporate businesses' own websites. We record the company name, public business email, source page, relevance notes and review date. We check the corporate identity and contact purpose before sending, and hold unverified or unsuitable contacts out of the campaign. This campaign does not use named employee email addresses or target sole traders or ordinary partnerships.

In business correspondence, we may also receive a name, role, work contact details and the information you choose to send us. If we obtain personal information about you from a public website or another source, we tell you the specific source and the categories of information when we provide our privacy information. We do this within a reasonable period and no later than one month after obtaining it, or at our first communication if earlier, unless an applicable exception allows otherwise. A publicly listed address is not evidence of consent to marketing.

Where permitted, we use legitimate interests to introduce relevant products to corporate businesses. We rely on this basis only after assessing necessity, relevance and the balance with your rights and reasonable expectations. Where consent is required, including for sole traders and certain partnerships unless an applicable exception applies, we need that consent before sending marketing.

You can object to direct marketing at any time. Reply to a marketing email or contact support@dot2.co.uk and ask us to stop. We stop marketing to contacts who object and keep a minimal suppression record to avoid contacting them again. Where we rely on consent, you can withdraw it at any time.

Sharing and retention

We use Google Workspace to send, receive and store business correspondence, including message content, email addresses and other contact information. Google's processing is governed by the applicable Workspace agreement and Cloud Data Processing Addendum.

Correspondence can be processed outside the United Kingdom, including in the United States. Google's published transfer arrangements provide a UK Extension to the EU-US Data Privacy Framework route for covered transfers to Google LLC in the United States. For other restricted transfers, contractual safeguards, including the applicable Standard Contractual Clauses, apply where required by those terms. Contact support@dot2.co.uk for more information or a copy of applicable safeguards.

If you place an order, we may share the details needed to fulfil it with production, delivery and accounting providers. We may also disclose information where required by law.

We keep enquiry and quote records for as long as needed to handle the request and reasonable follow-up. For orders, retention also depends on accounting requirements and the need to resolve disputes. We review business outreach records for relevance and accuracy at least every 90 days and remove contact information when it is no longer needed for its stated purpose. We keep only the minimal suppression information needed to respect an objection and avoid contacting that address again. You can ask us about the retention criteria for your particular information.

Your rights

Depending on the circumstances, you can ask to access, correct or delete your personal information, restrict or object to its use, or receive a portable copy. Contact support@dot2.co.uk to exercise a right or raise a privacy complaint. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO).

Our internal advertising tool

The Dot2. advertising profile is for an internal tool that manages Dot2.’s own Amazon advertising. The tool does not collect personal data.

This website

Dot2. does not set cookies or use analytics, advertising trackers or browser storage on this website. Quote details are not submitted to a website server or a third-party form service.

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages, which logs visitors’ IP addresses for security. For the hosting provider’s handling of visitor data, see the GitHub Privacy Statement. If you follow a product link, Amazon’s privacy notice applies to your use of Amazon.
